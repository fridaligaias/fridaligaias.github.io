womyn.network Receives Ignite Fund Support

womyn.network has received support from the Ignite Fund, marking an important step in moving the project beyond a final-year proof of concept and into its next stage of development.

This project is very close to me. womyn.network began as a security-first social web application centred on women's safety and privacy online, and it has given me space to explore the intersection of web engineering, AI, and protective design in a way that feels both technically challenging and genuinely meaningful.

The funding will help support the next stage of the platform's development and technical infrastructure. I am grateful for that support, not only because it helps practically, but because it recognises the value of continuing to build tools that take online safety, privacy, and participation seriously.

At a recent networking event, I also received thoughtful feedback from Francois Roubert and saw encouraging interest in the project from Luba Prout, Ph.D. Those conversations helped sharpen how I think about the next stage of womyn.network, especially around development priorities, public communication, and the path from prototype to a more mature platform.

The next phase is about continuing to build carefully: refining the product, strengthening the technical foundations, and making sure the platform grows in a way that stays aligned with its original purpose.

Onwards and upwards.
